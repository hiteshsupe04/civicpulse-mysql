Help & support

Find answers or contact the CivicPulse support team.

How do I report a vehicle?
Open Report violation, enter the vehicle number, location and violation details, then submit your report.
How are rewards calculated?
Each verified vehicle report provides 10 coins. 10 coins are equal to ₹10.
How can I redeem rewards?
Open Redeem rewards, enter your UPI ID and select the number of coins you want to redeem.
Can I see my vehicle complaint history?
Yes. My complaints contains your submitted vehicle numbers, violation types, locations and statuses.
